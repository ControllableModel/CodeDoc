   one exception: it is always emitted**, because an absent section reads as missing content
   rather than as "not applicable". **`## Errors` is the
   one exception and is always emitted**: an absent section reads as missing content rather
   than as "not applicable", so state the negative explicitly.
2. **`## Behaviour` is mandatory and is where Completeness is earned.** It must cover all
   five families — what it does, inputs, outputs, failures, side effects — each on its own
   line. When the source does not show a family, say so in one clause rather than omitting
   the line: a reader must be able to tell "absent" from "not documented".
3. **Never restate the signature in prose.** The parameter table is the reference; the
   Behaviour section describes meaning, not the argument list again.
4. **No invented facts.** Every claim must be traceable to the source above, the evidence
   blocks, or the metadata. Do not state a version, release date, author, license or
   benchmark, and do not describe behaviour the source does not show.
5. **Length: 250–400 words for a function or method, 500–700 for a class.** Behaviour is
   where the length goes; padding inside tables is not.
6. **One content type:** this artefact is **Reference** content. Procedural material appears
   as a short numbered sequence only inside Getting Started.
7. **Structured data goes in tables**: parameters, returns, errors.
8. **Heading depth is H1 then H2 only.** No H3, no skipped levels.
