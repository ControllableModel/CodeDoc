## ISO Documentation Standards (8 Dimensions, 26 Requirements)

### 1. Content Quality
- **Correctness:** Accurate, version-consistent, error-free
- **Consistency:** Unified terminology, format, UI element naming
- **Comprehensibility:** Clear language, user-friendly vocabulary
- **Conciseness:** No redundancy, minimalist, task-focused
- **Completeness:** Full coverage of tasks, concepts, steps, troubleshooting

### 2. Information Structure
- **Modularity:** Independent, reusable, self-contained topics
- **Structured by Function:** Organize by Concept → Guide → Reference → Troubleshooting
- **Navigability:** TOC, index, search, explicit bidirectional links
- **Metadata:** Include audience, version, info type for each topic

### 3. Format & Presentation
- **Consistent Format:** Unified style guide for fonts, colors, icons, warnings
- **Legibility:** Appropriate font size, contrast, avoid all-caps/excessive styling
- **Illustrations & Media:** Clear graphics, culturally neutral, video controls
- **Interactive Elements:** Consistent representation of shortcuts, gestures, variables

### 4. Accessibility
- **Multi-modal Support:** Text alternatives, screen reader support, high-contrast
- **Device Independence:** Clear noun-based titles, beginner-friendly
- **Accessibility Features:** Document software's accessibility capabilities

### 5. Internationalization & Localization
- **Cultural Neutrality:** Avoid culture-specific content, use international formats
- **Translation-Friendly:** Simple sentences, consistent terms, separable text in graphics
- **Space Consideration:** Reserve expansion space for longer languages

### 6. Usability & User Experience
- **User-Centered Design:** Task-based, role-based, context-sensitive help
- **Testing & Feedback:** Usability testing, continuous improvement
- **Search & Indexing:** Full-text search, Boolean, filters, synonyms

### 7. Safety & Compliance
- **Safety Information:** Prominent Danger/Warning/Caution with standard signal words
- **Legal & Copyright:** Include copyright, trademarks, licenses, regulatory compliance

### 8. Maintenance & Updates
- **Version Control:** Sync with software versions, use CM systems
- **Change Log:** Mark new/modified content, provide update notes
