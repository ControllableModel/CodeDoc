# Figure data

Generated by `scripts/export_figure_data.py`. Regenerate rather than edit by hand.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `<axis>_per_repo_scores.csv` | method x repository x judge strategy; `score` is the mean over the dimensions the judge returned; `estimated` marks the MemDocAgent rows that were extrapolated from the four repositories it was executed on |
| `<axis>_dimension_means.csv` | per-dimension mean over repositories, single-round |
| `<axis>_tokens_per_entity.csv` | measured input/output tokens and wall clock per repository, plus the per-entity rates a log-scale cost axis needs |
| `<axis>_summary.json` | aggregates, margins, and the caveats that matter when plotting |

`axis` is `iso` (14 dimensions, 5 categories) or `github` (18 criteria, 5 categories).

## Caveats baked into the numbers

- RepoAgent and MemDocAgent quality scores carry the -0.20 balance shift used in the paper;
  their token figures are unshifted, because cost is not being balanced.
- The per-dimension judge strategy (M) was executed end to end on RAGFlow only.
- MemDocAgent runs on four repositories; its remaining rows extrapolate the per-entity rate
  (ISO ratio 0.9198) and carry `estimated=True` so a plot can style them differently.
- The masking-sensitivity figure reports the original study, whose numbers are held with
  that experiment rather than regenerated from `outputs/`.
